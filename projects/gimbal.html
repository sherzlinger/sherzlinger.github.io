Two-Axis Camera Gimbal

Design study

A direct-drive two-axis pointing platform for the optical communication work above — built for precise tracking, not handheld stabilization. Currently a costed design: parts are selected, nothing is ordered.

  • Direct-drive BLDC on both axes — no gearbox, so there's no backlash to fight in the pointing loop.
  • Specified around paired ODrive S1 controllers, M8325s 100 KV motors and Jetson Orin Nano compute, with CAN between them.
  • Blocked on payload mass against cantilever load — the constraint that sets every other number in the design, and the reason nothing is ordered yet.
