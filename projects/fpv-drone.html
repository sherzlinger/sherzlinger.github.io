Mechanical assembly, electronics, tuning

FPV Drone Build

Complete

A 5-inch FPV freestyle quadcopter assembled from a parts kit — full mechanical build, every ESC, FC, VTX and motor connection soldered by hand.

  • Configured and tuned the flight controller in Betaflight: PID tuning for acro mode, rates and expo dialed in for freestyle, OSD layout, failsafe configuration and motor direction mapping.
  • Originally flew a TBS Crossfire receiver against a Spektrum transmitter — a non-native pairing that needed telemetry passthrough set up so RSSI, battery voltage and link quality could route back to the radio.
  • Since migrated the radio link to ExpressLRS, which trades that bridging work for an open, natively-supported link.
  • Significant debugging across the Crossfire LUA scripts and Betaflight's serial port mapping to make that link work.
  • Final result: full acro-mode flight with live telemetry feedback to the radio.
