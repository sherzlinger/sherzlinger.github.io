Team project · 10:1 reducer on an ODrive M8325s

Cycloidal Drive

Built

A 10:1 cycloidal reducer for a pan axis, 3D printed and mounted directly on an ODrive M8325s brushless motor. The geometry comes from a parametric generator that builds every part as solid CAD, checks the assembly for collisions through a full turn of the input, and exports toleranced print files.

  • 11 ring rollers (624 bearings), 1.5 mm eccentricity, a Ø122 mm housing, and 8 output pins running on 693 bearings; 6808 bearings carry the input and output.
  • Built in place on the motor: the eccentric hub bolts to the rotor face, the output reuses the motor's own 4 × M3 pattern, so the pan plate moves from the motor to the reducer unchanged.
  • The design passed both a fast swept clearance check (every 1° of a full input turn) and an exact solid-boolean check before printing; press fits were tuned on printed test coupons.
  • The animation is the generator's own model of this build: ten input turns, one output turn.
